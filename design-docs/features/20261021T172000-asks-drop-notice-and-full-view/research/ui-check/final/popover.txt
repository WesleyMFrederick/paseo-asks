Keep carts in Redis or Postgres?
2h
Why decide now: the checkout rework writes the cart on every click, and the storage choice shapes the CartStore interface all of it codes against.
What's true today: carts live in the session cookie, capped at 4 KB, so a long cart is silently cut short:
res.cookie('cart', JSON.stringify(cart)) // throws past 4 KB in some browsers
Options:
•
Postgres (recommended). Already running, transactional with orders, nothing new to operate.
•
Redis. Faster writes and built-in expiry, but one more service on call, and carts could vanish on eviction.
•
Keep the cookie. No work, and the 4 KB cut-off stays.
Benchmarks in the spike PR.
